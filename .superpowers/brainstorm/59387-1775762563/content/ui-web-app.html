💬 Your Negotiation Script

Brake Pads + Rotors · ₹14,000 → Target: ₹4,500
YOU SAY:
"I've done some research and the standard rate for brake pads and rotor service on a Honda City in Pune is ₹3,500–5,500. Can you help me understand what's driving your quote to ₹14,000?"
IF THEY SAY: "Parts are expensive"
They may be quoting OEM prices for aftermarket parts, or inflating part costs.
YOUR RESPONSE:
"Are these OEM Honda parts or aftermarket? Can I see the part packaging and the price label? I'd like to verify the part number."
IF THEY SAY: "Labour is high"
Standard labour for this job is 1–1.5 hours.
YOUR RESPONSE:
"Can you show me the labour rate card? What's the hourly rate, and how many hours does this job take? Standard is about 1 hour."
IF THEY SAY: "Rotors need full replacement"
Rotors only need replacement if wear is below manufacturer spec (usually 1mm).
YOUR RESPONSE:
"Can you measure the rotor thickness with a caliper and show me? The Honda spec is minimum 1mm. If it's above that, resurfacing should be enough."
🚪 IF THEY CAN'T ANSWER ANY OF THESE — THAT IS YOUR SIGNAL TO LEAVE

🧠 Negotiation Tactics

📊 Anchor Low

Start at ₹3,500 (bottom of fair range). They'll counter. You'll meet around ₹4,500 — which is still fair.

🔍 Ask for Proof

Legitimate mechanics can show part prices, labor rates, and wear measurements. If they refuse, it's a red flag.

⏰ Remove Urgency

If they say "this is urgent," ask: "What happens if I drive it home and come back tomorrow?" Brake pads don't fail instantly.

🏪 Mention Alternatives

"I've got quotes from two other garages in the ₹4,000–5,000 range. Can you match that?"
